# The Angels' Wing

Archive of *The Angels' Wing*, an NFT art podcast that ran from March 2022 to September 2023.

We were a community of independent NFT collectors and artists. Every Sunday we held a public space and talked with emerging artists about their artwork, web3, NFT pricing strategies, marketplaces, and much more. We also showcased their work in a virtual 3D gallery.

**Feed:** https://feed.theangelswing.art/the-angels-wing.xml

66 episodes, roughly 46 hours. The show has ended. The feed stays online and the episodes stay where they have always been, so existing subscriptions keep working. There just won't be anything new.

Hosted by the OG team: Casper, Ethspresso, Yune, Johannes, and Rabble.
